Physical Hardware Specifications (Make-A-Thon Prototype)

ESP32 IoT sensor node peripheral mapping
FIRMWARE v1.4.2-TRACE
Microcontroller: ESP32 NodeMCU (Xtensa 240MHz)
Magnetic Sensor: Normally-Open Reed Switch (GPIO 4)
Lock Motor: SG90 9g Micro Servo 50Hz PWM (GPIO 18)
RFID Reader: RC522 13.56MHz SPI (Pins 23,19,18,5,22)
Matrix Keypad: 4x4 Membrane Keypad (GPIO Rows/Cols)
Audio Siren: 5V Active Piezo Buzzer (GPIO 19)
Indicator LEDs: Dual Green / Red Status LEDs (GPIO 2 & 15)
Power Supply: Regulated 5V 2A DC / Micro-USB
